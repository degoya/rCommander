## 0.1.0

The first release: Dockerfiles and Containerfiles coloured like an editor - found by their name
(`Dockerfile`, `Dockerfile.dev`, `Containerfile`) or the extensions `.dockerfile` and
`.containerfile` - with instructions, flags, variables, strings, parser directives, continued
lines and heredocs in their own colours, and the shell form of `RUN` in the shell viewer's.
