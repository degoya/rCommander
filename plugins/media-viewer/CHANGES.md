# Changes

## 0.1.0

The first release: the duration, codecs, tracks and tags of audio files (MP3, FLAC, Ogg, Opus,
M4A, WAV, AIFF, APE, WavPack, AAC) and video files (MP4, MOV, 3GP, MKV, WebM), the embedded cover
as a picture (switched off on the Plugins page), and the EXIF of TIFF, camera raw (DNG, CR2, NEF,
ARW) and HEIC photos, with the GPS position in decimal degrees - as a tree and as text.
